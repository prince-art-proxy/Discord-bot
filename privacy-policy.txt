Seahorse AI – Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Introduction
Seahorse AI (“the Bot”, “we”, “us”) is a Discord bot. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using Seahorse AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect
Seahorse AI may collect the following information when you use the bot:

• Discord User Data: User ID, username, display name, avatar, and server (guild) ID when you interact with the bot.
• Message Content: Only the content of messages that trigger bot commands or are necessary for the bot to function.
• Server Information: Server ID, server name, and channel IDs where the bot is used.
• Usage Data: Command usage statistics (which commands are used and how often) for improving the bot.

We do not collect:
• Email addresses
• IP addresses
• Payment information
• Private messages (DMs) unless you specifically interact with the bot in DMs

3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:
• Provide and operate the bot’s features
• Respond to your commands
• Improve the bot’s performance and fix bugs
• Prevent abuse and enforce our rules

4. Data Storage & Retention
• Data is stored only as long as necessary to provide the service.
• We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
• Some temporary data may be cached in memory while the bot is running and is deleted when the bot restarts or the data is no longer needed.

5. Data Sharing
We do not share your data with third parties except:
• When required by law
• To protect the rights, safety, or property of Seahorse AI, its users, or the public

6. Third-Party Services
Seahorse AI runs on Discord’s platform. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

7. Your Rights
You can:
• Stop using the bot at any time
• Request deletion of data related to your user ID (contact the bot owner)
• Remove the bot from any server you manage

8. Children’s Privacy
Seahorse AI is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, contact the bot owner through Discord.
